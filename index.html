FAQ

Quick answers

What platforms do the apps support?

Bit Q Pro and DitCopy are native macOS apps. Time Bridge is built for iPhone (and runs on compatible iPad / Mac where Apple allows).

How do purchases work?

Bit Q Pro is a one-time purchase on Gumroad. DitCopy is a one-time Mac App Store purchase. Time Bridge is free to download, with an optional one-time Pro unlock — no subscription.

What’s free vs Pro in Time Bridge?

Free covers core planning with modest limits (people, suggestions, templates, and converter zones). Pro unlocks higher limits plus calendar tools, reminders, and Lock Screen Live Activity sharing. Restore Purchases is in Settings.

Where can I get help or read privacy info?

DitCopy: Support · Privacy. Time Bridge: Support · Privacy. For Bit Q Pro, use the contact options on your Gumroad receipt.
